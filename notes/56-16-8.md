# [[56,16,8]] - general stabilizer subgroup code

## Direction & hypothesis

Study the dimension–distance tradeoff obtained by selecting stabilizer subgroups and changing sparse presentations.

## What was searched

Rank40 codimension-two subgroup of the literal FarLab public [[56,14,8]] 2BGA stabilizer. Selected cross-sector coefficient pairs (14,20) and (15,15) are identified, followed by a literal same-group basis exchange.

The displayed presentation has maximum check weight 16, maximum qubit degree 9, 40 checks and total incidence 412.

## Evidence trail

The JSON contains weight-8 logical witnesses. Distance confidence: P: upper_bound.

## Reproduction
