# [[717,8,25]] — five local reductions of a planar tile code

## Direction & hypothesis

The published `codes/722-8-25.json` is a weight-8, single-layer 2D tile
code with eight logical qubits and explicit weight-25 logical operators.
Local stabilizer pivots can remove physical qubits while retaining its
checks, layout class, and logical witnesses. We sought a smaller `n` in
that board cell. The reported distance is a witness-backed upper bound.

## What was searched

Four compatible pivots reduced the 722-qubit code to a staged 718-qubit
ancestor. A fifth single-pivot scan found 95 structural 717-qubit
variants preserving check weight at most 8, radius at most 4, and at
least one inherited weight-25 logical with neither side below 25.
Sixty-one retained `k=8`, Tanner and
stabilizer-block connectivity, and valid logicals. Projection of 283
light ancestor logicals, including stabilizer-row translates and RIS
witnesses lifted from the children, refuted seven variants with
validated weight-24 logicals. A 10,000-trial, pair-depth-8 screen of
the 59 initial survivors found one of those refutations. We tested
X17/pivot291, Z375/pivot350, and Z717/pivot288 at 100,000 trials and
pair depth 512: X17 and Z717 returned weight 25; Z375 returned a
validated weight-24 X logical. We selected X17/pivot291.

## Evidence trail

The unchanged trusted candidate gate passed the X17/pivot291 JSON with
seed 894136645. It checked CSS commutation, `n=717`, `k=8`, maximum
check weight 8, a connected one-layer radius-4 layout, the two
weight-25 logicals, deduplication, and an 8,000-trial RIS refutation
search. It marked the weight-8, local-2d-single cell as advancing by
lower `n`. An independent five-pivot reconstruction from
`codes/722-8-25.json` matched the submitted checks and coordinates.
The Z witness matches the published parent's projection; the X
witness descends from a validated RIS result on the 719-qubit ancestor.

On the exact 717-qubit matrices, accelerated RIS found no logical below
25 in 10,000 pair-depth-8 trials (seed 725011991), 100,000
pair-depth-512 trials (seed 1229101991), 1,000,000 pair-depth-512
trials (seed 1230001991), and 1,000,000 pair-depth-4096 trials (seed
4814001991). Every returned logical was checked against those
matrices. The 718-qubit ancestor also returned weight 25 in two
independent million-trial runs (pair depths 512 and 4096).

**`d≤25` is a witness-backed upper bound.** The bounded searches do not
prove a minimum distance of 25. The witnessed score is
`8 × 25² / 717 = 6.974`, versus 6.925 for the 722-qubit parent.

## Dead ends

The Z375/pivot350 fifth reduction found a weight-24 X logical at
100,000 pair-depth-512 trials (seed 1229237850). Several other 717-qubit
variants found weight-24 logicals by short RIS or projection. The
remaining shallow-screen survivors have not been deeply confirmed.

## Reproduction

Start from `codes/722-8-25.json`. At each step, XOR the named pivot
row into every other same-side check containing the target qubit;
delete the pivot row and qubit, shorten opposite-side checks, delete
that coordinate, and renumber surviving qubits. In order:

1. Z row 289, support `{0,362,363}`, target qubit 0.
2. Z row 289, support `{1,18,361,380,381}`, target qubit 18.
3. Z row 350, support `{356,357,716}`, target qubit 357.
4. Z row 353, support `{356,357,715,716}`, target qubit 357.
5. X row 291, support `{16,17,35,393,412,413}`, target qubit 17.

Project the published Z witness through all five pivots. The X witness
in the submitted JSON comes from accelerated RIS on the 719-qubit
intermediate and is projected through the final two pivots. The JSON
contains the final checks, coordinates, and both witness supports.
