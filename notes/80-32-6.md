# [[80,32,6]] - rank-24 CSS subgroup of a public 2BGA code

## Direction & hypothesis

Increase the encoded dimension of the public [[80,24,8]] 2BGA code by retaining a smaller stabilizer subgroup, while keeping maximum check weight 10. The parent was contributed by @FarLab in [PR #3773](https://github.com/unitaryfoundation/qldpc-challenge/pull/3773).

## What was searched

The selected subgroup keeps 24 original X rows and 24 original Z rows. Their zero-based row masks are X = 877032374220 and Z = 879599236095. Both selected sector ranks are 24, giving k = 32. The displayed basis has maximum check weight 10, maximum qubit degree 7, 48 checks and total incidence 480.

## Evidence trail

The submission tool searches for logical witnesses and verifies the generated JSON. Its sector distance claims are witness-backed upper bounds. The retained exact-distance research check excludes all nonzero centralizer words through weight 5: 3,241 syndromes for errors through weight 2 are distinct, and all 82,160 triples avoid that set. Genuine weight-6 quotient witnesses establish exact distance 6 separately.

## Dead ends

The fixed-parent degree-6 census leaves 100 admissible binary selections, all with sector ranks 20 and 20. None spans the required 24 plus 24 ranks; the selected degree-7 presentation avoids that obstruction.

## Tools

Recorded workflow configuration: GPT-6 Astra + GPT-6.1 Sol. The official qldpc submit run used 20,000 RIS trials per sector with seed 0, followed by its independent refutation check.
