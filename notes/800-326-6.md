## What was searched

G=C5 semidirect C16, normal form a^i b^j, with b a b^-1=a^2. Index i+5*j. Multiplication (i,j)(k,l)=(i+2^j*k mod5,j+l mod16). L_g[t,u]=1 iff u=g*t; R_h[t,u]=1 iff u=t*h. A=[I+L5,I+L6,I+L28]; B=[I+R5,I+R16,I+R19], addition mod2. There are nine VV(k,j) blocks indexed 3*k+j for k,j in 0..2, followed by one CC block, all of size80. X(j) has A(k) on VV(k,j) and B(j) on CC. Z(k) has B(j)^T on VV(k,j) and A(k)^T on CC. Keep first independent original rows, giving 237 checks per sector.
